About the fuller story

with the department of quality control.

Marketing is helping someone fall in love — with a place, a brand, a version of themselves. Every piece in this portfolio is built to do that one thing, across whatever medium the moment calls for.

A marketer who designs, a designer who writes.

By title, I’m the in-house marketing lead for Sea 2 Sky Living — the Whistler & Squamish real-estate practice of Rob Palm PREC. In practice, I’m the whole creative department: every deck, brochure, report, campaign, film and caption in this portfolio was designed, written and shipped by one set of hands.

None of it was made for a pitch. It was made to perform — to win a listing, fill an open house, earn the next signup — and then to do it again next week. Real estate is the proving ground, not the boundary: the same craft moves to any brand that wants one voice everywhere.

Before marketing there was philosophy — a UVic BA that became a work habit: figure out what a thing actually means before deciding how it should look. I think with my hands, learn tools by using them in production, and sweat the details people feel but never mention.
