The Print Room: ink, paper and inboxes.

The brand’s longer conversations: brochures that ride in glove boxes, cards that get pinned to fridges, and publications that land in inboxes four times a year. Everything here is designed and written by the same hand, which is why the words and the layout never argue.

Exhibit 01 · Tri-fold brochures two towns, one hand

One brochure system, two markets: Whistler gets the alpine lake panorama, Squamish gets the Chief. Same grid, same voice, different mountain; the point of a brand system is that both are unmistakably the same practice. Client quotes ride along on every panel.

Exhibit 02 · The card series small kindnesses

Client care in red, black and one A-frame cabin. The thank-you card draws Black Tusk in a single continuous line; the birthday card gives the bears party hats. Small pieces, but they’re the ones clients pin up, which makes them the longest-running ad the brand owns.
